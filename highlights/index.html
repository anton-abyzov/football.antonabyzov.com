TwoTerritory-Maxline against Lidselmash · Vysshaya Liga RB futsal · SK MAPID

Scoring against the reigning Belarus champion.
Minsk, 17 February 2019.

Vysshaya Liga RB futsal · Minsk · 17 Feb 2019 Play at the goalLoads YouTube on click

My own channel, the passage into my goal and the board turning over.

The official protocol reads 2:3 - Абызов (13, пас - Попович) (Abyzov, 13th minute, assist Popovich), plus my pre-assist on the equaliser at four minutes. I wore shirt 3.

Lidselmash were the reigning Belarus champions, and three months earlier they had played the Elite Round of the 2018/19 UEFA Futsal Champions League against Kairat Almaty and Chrudim. We lost 5-11. They were the better team by a distance and that is the honest version. The point is the level, not the scoreline.

One thing about the footage: the surviving video jumps the match clock from 12:47 to 12:50, so the strike itself is not in it. What you see is the passage into it and the board turning over to 2-3.

ThreeAFL Belarus IT Cup final · ISsoft 3-2 Luch

The IT Cup final equaliser.
Minsk, 12 August 2021.

AFL Belarus IT Cup final · Minsk · 12 Aug 2021 Play at the equaliserLoads YouTube on click

Full final on the AFL Belarus stream. The jump lands four seconds before the strike.

Nought-one down with twenty minutes left. Their keeper claimed a cross, we broke three against three, the ball rolled across to me and I hit it first time into the top corner. Open play, first time, no dead ball. We won it 3-2.

The working folder for this cut is still named after a free kick. It was not one. That word does not belong in any title, caption or file name for this goal, and it is not used here.

What a strike. What a goal. … Tell me this guy’s surname. … He put it beautifully into the top corner, no chance for the keeper. … That is the jewel of this final, the real jewel.

Commentary, AFL Belarus streamTranslated from the Russian off the broadcast audio. The scorer is in the ISsoft white shirt with navy sleeves, number 23, which is the number I wore that day.
