GoldNantes, July 2009

European champion, 2009.

IBSA B2/B3 European Championship, Nantes, France, with the Belarus national futsal team for the visually impaired. Eleven teams, July 2009. Belarus beat Ukraine 4-0 in the decisive match.

I am not the only person who says I was in that squad. sb.by, a Belarusian national daily, previewed the team on 29 July 2009 and called me the squad's newest signing, noting in passing that two of us were programmers. The same piece describes the pre-tournament medical commission, which is the thing that later kept me out of Antalya.

I had minus ten glasses, but to take part I had to pass every eye test on special equipment. I passed, I qualified, and we won the Euro.

Anton AbyzovMy own written account of the qualification.
  • 18 November 2009 Master of Sport of the Republic of Belarus

    Futsal among the visually impaired, conferred by the Ministry of Sport and Tourism four and a half months after Nantes. The booklet itself is photographed; it names the discipline and the date of conferral.

  • 16 December 2009 Commendation of the President of the Republic of Belarus

    By order signed by Alexander Lukashenko for that win. Eleven players and the coach, Anatoly Usenko (Анатолий Усенко), and my name is listed first.

  • April 2011 · Antalya, Turkey The world title I was not cleared for

    IBSA World Games, Antalya. Belarus took the B2/B3 world title, their fifth. The medical commission did not clear me, so I did not travel. It is on this board because it is the tournament people assume I played.

  • June 2012 · Kayseri, Turkey 2012 European Championship, Kayseri: gold medal with the Belarus squad

    IBSA B2/B3 European Championship, 4 to 9 June 2012, won by Belarus. I travelled with the squad and I was given the gold medal with them, on a European Champions ribbon, photographed on the final day. No minutes and no goals are claimed here.

  • May 2015 · Borisov Still in the national-team picture

    Belarus against France in Borisov, on film.

Gold on this page means a real title, medal or order won by me. The 2012 Kayseri line is printed plain on purpose: the medal is mine, the tournament is the squad's.

Club I builtItransition, 2010 to 2014

A city title, then the national top division.

I founded the Itransition company futsal team with colleagues and captained it. First outing, 2010: second of eight at the annual IT Spartakiad.

Completely unexpectedly, for everyone and even for ourselves, we took first place in the city championship.

Anton AbyzovOn winning the Minsk city mini-football championship. Interview with devby.io, 22 October 2012.

That title is what got us invited into the Belarus national top futsal division, where we played as Itransition-BGUFK in alliance with the Belarusian State University of Physical Culture for a base, a methodology and squad depth. Ninth of fifteen in 2011. Fifth in 2012.

I was its founder, its captain and its manager, which in practice meant the budget and the fixture list as well as the pivot position. The debut in that division, on 11 September 2010, was a 1-2 defeat. The first win came a fortnight later: 5-3 against Lidselmash on 25 September 2010. The BGUFK cooperation agreement was signed on 8 August 2011, for the season that finished fifth.

Fifth in the national Higher League, not a city league. And the city title is a futsal title, not a football one.

Season volume from the same era, from protocols I transcribed myself: roughly 24 goals across the 2012/13 Minsk city championship, with 27 full protocols preserved and my name in 18 of them, including five goals in one match (Itransition 15-4 VRZ, 13 November 2012). In 2013/14 I was second in the team's own scoring list with 23.

ScoutingSmall pitch

How I play on a small pitch.

Pivot hold-up play, penetrating passes, and both feet. In futsal a player has to use both feet to receive and to pass. Without it, everything you do is predictable.

  • Receipt · 2008 rubric Two-footed by doctrine

    I wrote the both-feet rule in a coaching note years before anyone asked me about it, and pivot play was already graded a strength in my own ten-skill rubric in 2008.

  • Receipt · match archive Set pieces are mine to design as well as to hit

    Free-kick goals in December 2021, December 2024 and February 2025, and three separate 2026 games where I called a corner routine and we scored it first time.

  • Receipt · match notes And when it goes wrong I go in goal

    I played a whole second half as flying keeper on 27 February 2021, and on 16 October 2021 I went in properly after we conceded six, and still scored in a four-against-four.
