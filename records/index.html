SixMine, not a league's

What is not sourced here.

Three kinds of number on this site are mine rather than a league's, and they are labelled as such wherever they appear.

  • My own count Assist counts for 2026

    The league page publishes 13 goals in 10 games. The nine assists are my own count.

  • My own archive The personal match archive

    104 logged matches, 147 goals, 54 assists, 20 best-player awards since 2012. That is my own archive, and plenty of games were never filmed or logged, so it is a floor.

  • My own account Anything marked "my own account"

    Such as the assist against Mako SC in 2023, and the penalty and crossbar contest in Miami. Those photos sit in my own archive, not on a league page. Two stories that were in this category have since come out of it altogether: the Bulgaria accuracy contest, because the photograph behind it turned out to be a 2016 resort collage, and the Slovakia tournament, because the only frames of it were forwarded through Viber with the EXIF stripped and cannot be dated.

Nothing on this site is published unless it is either on one of the pages above or clearly labelled as my own record. Recorded figures are recorded, not career totals.
