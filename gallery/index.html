Gallery.
Six eras, dated.

Every frame here carries a date, a place and where it came from. Where a photograph should exist and does not, the slot stays on the page and says so. I would rather show you the gap than fill it with something I cannot stand behind.

1 · Baranovichi 2 · Minsk 3 · National team 4 · Minsk halls 5 · Germany 6 · Miami

Build rule for this page: nothing goes in above 300 KB, nothing goes in without a caption and alt text, and the originals stay on my own drive. Two files from the Baller League arena are excluded by name because my daughter is in frame.

Era 1Baranovichi, the start

Where somebody first put me on a team.

I grew up in Baranovichi, in the Brest region of Belarus, and I went to Lyceum No 1. The football started there, in the school hall and on the city pitches, with a teacher who kept entering us for tournaments.

  • School football, 1990s No photograph from those years is on either drive or in the vault. If it exists it is a print in a family album in Baranovichi.
  • Lyceum No 1, the building Not shot yet. It needs a trip home or a photograph used with permission.

Era 2Minsk, 2002 to 2016

BSUIR, then Itransition.
Wooden floor, green shirt, number 4.

University in Minsk, then the company team. The squad photograph and the cups come out of my own archive, and the two frames after them are lifted from my 2014/15 season compilation, which is the oldest footage of me that exists anywhere online. The titles burned into those two are mine, from that edit.

  • Minsk city championship The trophy and the medals from the city title with Itransition are not photographed anywhere I can find.

Era 3National team, 2009 and 2012

Nantes, and then Kayseri.

The 2009 European title in Nantes is the one with a medal, a certificate and a presidential commendation behind it. Turkey in 2012 was a trip I was on with the squad. That is not a personal title and it is not filed as one anywhere on this site.

  • Presidential commendation, 2009 The document and the ceremony. The image attached to my own note is a dead link, so the paper itself has to be rephotographed.
  • Bulgaria, the accuracy contest Checked in round two and withdrawn. The photograph behind that story turned out to be a 2016 resort collage with a football simulator booth in it, which is not a record of a contest, so nothing from it goes on this page.
  • Slovakia, tournament group photo Two frames exist in my own Google Photos, both forwarded through Viber with the EXIF stripped, so neither can be dated or placed. Slovakia stays off this site until I can supply a date and a place.

The only surviving copy of the squad frame is 440 pixels wide and it runs here at that size, which is why it stays small next to everything around it. Its old filename says 2008. The championship was 2009.

Era 4Minsk halls, 2012 to 2026

The Higher League rooms, and the ones I still play in.

Big halls, small crowds, very good players. The top-flight years left me one frame, and it is scene setting rather than identification. Everything after it is recent Minsk futsal: my own EasyChamp side in the white kit, and the last group game of the 2026 Summer Cup, shot by the league.

  • Territory-Maxline, number 3 A team photograph or a match still with my number readable. Not on either drive.
  • Eight futsal clusters, 2012 to 2017 December 2012, February 2013, October 2014, January and February 2015, December 2015, February 2016, March 2017. All of it sits in Google Photos, none of it exported yet.

The 2019 frame is too wide to read a shirt number off, so take it as the room rather than as identification of me. The goal from the same match is on the highlight tape. The eight recent frames are a harder case: they are my club and my competition, the player in white looks like me to me, and until I say so out loud each one carries that caveat under it rather than a name.

Era 5Brandenburg, spring 2022

One match in Germany, fully documented.

I lived at Rangsdorf, south of Berlin, in the spring of 2022 and played for the local club. One afternoon of it is on the record in full, which is rare for anything in my career, and three of my own frames from that spring are below it.

  • Twenty seven more frames The rest of that afternoon is in Google Photos, ids recorded, export still blocked.

The coach made me man of the match that afternoon. That is his word and mine rather than something printed on the record below, so it stays here in the text.

Era 6Miami, 2022 to 2026

Florida.
Trophies, turf, and the arena I want to play in.

Everything from here on is from the last three years and most of it is on my own drive. Where a frame belongs to a club or to a league, the credit sits under it. The Baller League frames are the honest ones to look at hardest: I have been in that arena, and none of those photographs show me playing in it.

  • EasyChamp FC, shirt 8, in the game Match action from the 8v8, rather than the trophy shot. Today the only source is a frame grab off the Superliga stream.

Owner, coach and number 8 at EasyChamp FC. The USSL was won in 2024 with no post-season played, so there was no final to photograph.

HonestWhat this page cannot show yet

Three gaps, stated plainly.

  • Baller League No playing footage or photographs exist

    Every Baller League frame on this site is from the stand, the turf before a game, or my own camera. That is the whole point of the application.

  • Before 2023 Nothing from those years is on my drives

    Belarus, the national team, Itransition, university and Germany survive as YouTube uploads and as Google Photos albums. The photographs above are what came back from them.

  • Export The Google Photos download path is blocked

    Hundreds of frames across the Google Photos albums are identified by id and cannot be pulled automatically. That is the single biggest gap on this page, and it is mine to fix by hand.
