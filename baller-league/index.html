Field 9

Football Journey And Why You Are A Good Fit

I started at Lyceum No. 1 in Baranovichi, Belarus, where my PE teacher Evgeny Churilov took me to city tournaments. At university in Minsk I made the futsal team, then founded and captained the futsal side at Itransition. We won the Minsk city championship, were invited into the Belarus national top division, and finished 5th there in 2012. In 2009 I won the European Championship with the Belarus national team for the visually impaired, in Nantes. I have minus ten eyesight and had to pass a full medical qualification to be allowed to play. The President of Belarus signed a commendation for that win. In 2019 I scored against Lidselmash, the reigning national champion.

Then Germany, where I scored the only goal of a district match four weeks after arriving, and since August 2022, Miami. I own, coach and play in EasyChamp FC, the 2025 Superliga 8v8 champion. I coach Dade County FC with Artem Baranovskyi and I am on their UPSL Division 1 roster.

Why 6v6. Fifty of my fifty-three official goals came on a small pitch. At 41 I scored 13 in 10 games this summer and finished 2nd top scorer of the tournament. I play pivot, I pass through lines, I take the set pieces, I defend, and I have gone in goal when the team needed it.

And four people from my EasyChamp FC roster were in your final four in May 2026: Eugene Starikov, top scorer with 10 goals, head coach Pedro Pinheiro, Rapha Chiarella, and Artem Baranovskyi at centre-back for Super Ninos.
