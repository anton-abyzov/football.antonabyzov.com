ClubDade County FC, Miami

Announced as a coach in 2024. Back on the bench in 2026.

I came into the club in 2023 as a registered UPSL Premier Division player, and I played the 2023 and 2024 seasons for them before the bench. The earliest club footage I have is two Veo matches from February 2023.

Spring 2024, announced publicly as a coach, assisting head coach Artem Baranovskyi. The club put it as Artem being assisted by Anton, two people who know the club in and out. My own post that day called it an exciting day to be announced as a coach, laying the groundwork for a professional club mentality.

2024, the club won the USSL. The owner's message to the squad was that we had joined the USSL for greater control and flexibility, that it had turned out less competitive than expected, and that the squad had shown it was well above that level by winning the league with ease. First season at Traz Powell Stadium, last game 30 June 2024. No post-season was played, so there was no final and there is no trophy photograph from it.

17 August 2026, coaching again, with Artem. The owner's own line: Artem and Anton, coaches again.

2026 competitions: UPSL Division 1, Florida South South, for the first team, plus a USSL South Florida Fall 2026 reserve strand. I am registered as a player this season as well, which is how I like it.

Co-staff on record: Artem Baranovskyi, head coach in 2024 and coach again in 2026, a UEFA B licensed coach and a former professional, and Ewout Gouw, coach in 2023 and 2024.

We still play each other. A session on Sunny Isles turf, 24 May 2026, one of sixty-five takes off my own phone. Twelve seconds of a one against one.

Dade County FC is not Miami Dade FC. They are two different clubs and the records get mixed up online.

Set piecesFrom the folder

Two corner boards off my own drive.

Four corner diagrams are the coaching artefacts that survive as files. Two of them carry the actual instructions: numbered players, zonal blocks, a one against one on the edge of the box, and the runs drawn in.

The signals are coded on the pitch so nobody has to shout them: no hands is near post, one hand up is far post.

MethodIn my own words

Five seconds to win it back.

Every time we lose the ball we are aggressive, and we have five seconds to win it back.

Anton AbyzovThe heading on my own training note at Dade County FC.

The five points under it, as they are written:

  1. Strong pass in defence especially.
  2. Why am I doing this? What is the reason to move here, to stay here?
  3. If the opponent makes weak or sloppy passes, that is the indicator to start pressure.
  4. One of the most important and most complicated jobs is switching from defence to attack, above all when you are under pressure. The call "turn" should help you.
  5. We should always receive the ball facing the opponent's goal. There is no meaning in receiving the ball and moving towards your own keeper.

The habit at the end of every session, written in the same folder: thank every player with a handshake. Every one of them, every time.

What is on file next to the session plans: a full Russian translation of Pep Lijnders' Coaches' Voice masterclass on Liverpool 4-0 Barcelona, Antonio Conte's interval protocol, and a note on Jurgen Klopp. I translate the material I want the squad to hear, then I teach it.

CourtRules I still repeat

Head turning, nothing shameful about a pass backwards.

  • The head has to be turning constantly.
  • No shouting "where are you running, what are you doing" on the court. Ever.
  • There is absolutely nothing shameful about passing backwards.
  • The goalkeeper plays the whole court. You are not standing in the goal, you are playing. You are a full member of the team.
  • This is futsal, not football. I want false runs.
  • Wall passes are your bread. Our game is the counter.

Things I have built and run rather than talked about: a possession drill where you are not allowed to pass to anyone in your own half, the coded corner signals above, a five-block pre-game warm-up protocol, a written team rules and conduct document that ends with "when we enter a field we are a family" and "to be champions, we must act like champions", a rotation tied to attendance data with an under-23 blended into the first team, and matchday rosters of 20 to 24 players.

It is not just about showing what you can do with the ball at your feet. It is about showing who you are when the going gets tough.

    Minsk

The measurement method from that 2012 address is the thing that later became EasyChamp: everyone keeps records at home, how many shots you took, how many were on target, how many went in, drill by drill, with the load split into physical, technical and tactical. I was building a stats product before I knew that is what it was.

I am not putting a club name on the mid-2010s side. Whether it was Dorozhnik, MyWorld, or two different teams, is still open in my own archive, so it stays unnamed until I can prove it.

BoardEasyChamp FC

Owner, coach and number 8 of the same team.

I own EasyChamp FC, I coach it, and I play in it, shirt 8. Miami Superliga 8v8 champions, July 2025. After the final I allocated a 3,000 dollar prize pool across the squad myself, with a written per-player breakdown and categories for the leaders, the most persistent, and the important contributions.

Seven PS23 seasons on from the first fixture that is about 88 games, and the club's own records put me at 100 per cent attendance in every season that was measured, registered Midfield and Admin, captain. The title season read 11 wins, 1 draw, 4 defeats, 96-61, 34 points. PS23 stopped publishing per-player scorers after season C92, so I cannot tell you how many of those 96 goals were mine, and I am not going to estimate.

The lineup sheets put me consistently in the second line, right and deep, usually paired with Artem or Pedro, with a holding midfielder behind the front pair. I pick the shape, then I play inside it, which keeps me honest about whether it works.
