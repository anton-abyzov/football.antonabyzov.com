  2. 2011

    Corporate league to the national top division

    The team I founded and captained at Itransition started in a corporate IT league, won the Minsk city futsal championship, and that title took it into the Belarus national top futsal division, the Vysshaya Liga, in 2010. Top-division debut 11 September 2010, lost 1-2; first win a fortnight later, 5-3 against Lidselmash on 25 September. As Itransition-BGUFK it finished fifth in 2011-12, its best season.

    Minsk

ScoutingPlaying profile

A passer who breaks lines.

One line for a scout: a right-footed, two-footed ball-playing defender who turned into a small-sided creator and scorer. Pivot hold-up play, penetrating passes, long diagonals, and a finisher whose numbers multiply the moment the pitch shrinks.

  • Receipt · 2008 notebook Penetrating passes

    I wrote «обостряющие передачи» (obostryayushchie peredachi, penetrating passes) about my own game in a 2008 notebook, and that is still the part I trust most.

  • Receipt · 2008 rubric Pivot play

    Graded as a strength in my own ten-skill rubric in 2008, alongside shooting. Hold it with the sole, shield it, turn, restart the attack.

  • Receipt · 6 August 2026 Two-footed by doctrine

    In futsal a player has to use both feet to receive and to pass. Goal 4 on 6 August 2026 went in with my left.

  • Receipt · match archive Set pieces

    Free-kick goals in Minsk in December 2021 and in Miami in December 2024 and February 2025, plus three separate 2026 games where I called a corner routine and we scored it first time.

  • Receipt · DCFC match sheet Defensive work that is actually measured

    My own per-match sheet from a Dade County FC game against Tropical Sporting FC: 86% pass accuracy, 3 interceptions, 2 clearances.

  • Receipt · match archive Fills in anywhere, including goal

    I played a whole second half as flying keeper on 27 February 2021, and went in goal outright on 16 October 2021 and still scored.

BenchCoaching

Champion on the touchline, 2024.

I was announced as a coach of Dade County FC in spring 2024. Artem Baranovskyi and I took the side together. That season the club won the USSL, and the owner put it plainly to the squad: we were well above the level. No post-season was played, so there was no final. Artem and I are coaching there again since August 2026.

Before that, two coaching spells in Belarus futsal, in 2012 and around 2014-2015, with the session plans and post-match reviews still on file. And EasyChamp FC in Miami, where I am the owner, the coach and a player in the same team.

The line that matters to a 6v6 league: four people from my EasyChamp FC roster reached the Baller League USA final four on 16 May 2026. Eugene Starikov was the tournament's top scorer with 10 goals for Midnight Wizards, Pedro Pinheiro was their head coach, Rapha Chiarella scored 4, and Artem Baranovskyi played centre-back for Super Ninos, who won the final 6-2.

Every time we lose the ball we are aggressive, and we have five seconds to win it back. We always receive facing the opponent's goal.
