Football,
11v11 and 8v8.

Eleven-a-side is where I learned to defend and to pass, and where my goal numbers are modest and honest. Everything from 8v8 down is where they climb.

The single most useful line on my official record: three goals in 59 appearances of 11-a-side, against 50 goals in 8v8, 5v5 and futsal on the same page. Same player, same association, same career card. The pitch is the variable.

BrandenburgGermany, 10 April 2022

Four weeks in the country,
and the only goal of the game.

SV Rangsdorf 28 1-0 SV Siethen. Kreisfreundschaftsspiel, Kreis Dahme/Fläming, Brandenburg. Stadion Rangsdorfer See, Birkenallee 49, Rangsdorf. 45 spectators, kick-off 15:00, final whistle 16:40.

I scored the only goal and Rangsdorf's coach Michi named me man of the match. The official fixture page records the 1:0 and one goal event at 57 minutes.

SV Rangsdorf 28 e.V. plays German district amateur football, Kreisliga level. That is how this should be described, and it is enough on its own: I turned up in a country I had been in for four weeks and scored the winner.

BaranovichiWhere the outdoor game started

My first coach took me to the city tournaments.

My first football coach was Чурилов Евгений Владимирович (Churilov Evgeny Vladimirovich), the physical education teacher at Лицей №1 (Lyceum No 1) in Baranovichi, where I was a pupil. He is a football coach by qualification, a BGOIFK graduate who coached at the Luninets and Baranovichi youth sports schools before he taught at my school, and he has lectured at Baranovichi State University since 2004. He is the man who took me to the city tournaments, and that is where all of this begins.

The lyceum kept no sport archive that survives online, so there are no team photographs from those years to show you. His own university page does exist, and I have saved a copy of it in case it goes the way of the school's.

PitchWhy this page matters for 6v6

Three in 59, fifty in the small stuff.

Eleven-a-side taught me to defend, to pass and to read a game from the back. The scoring came the moment the pitch shrank, and the official card says so in one line: 3 goals in 59 games of 11v11, 50 goals in 8v8, 5v5 and futsal.

The 8v8 record is the closest thing I have to a 6v6 record: an ALF silver with 4 goals and 3 assists, an equaliser in a cup final, a Miami Superliga title, and about 88 games of 8v8 for my own club with 100 per cent attendance, still playing them at 41.
