Career, 1984 to 2026

Vertical ledger. Marginal year column, hairline rule between rows, accent bar on the rows that changed something. Every figure carries its source.

Two things to read before the numbers. First, recorded means recorded: the match archive I have kept since 2012 covers the games I logged, and plenty of others were never filmed or written down.

Second, Vysshaya Liga means three different competitions in my record and they are never blurred here.

Part one1984 to 2015

Baranovichi to Minsk.

  1. 1984

    Born

    16 November, in Baranovichi, Brest region, Belarus.

    Baranovichi

  2. 1990s

    School football in Baranovichi

    I went to Lyceum No. 1 in Baranovichi. My first football coach was Evgeny Vladimirovich Churilov (Чурилов Евгений Владимирович), the PE teacher there, and he took me to city tournaments. He had coached at the Luninets and Baranovichi youth sports schools before that, graduated from BGOIFK in 1976, and has lectured in the theory and practice of physical culture at Baranovichi State University since 2004.

    To 2002 · Lyceum

  3. 2002

    BSUIR, Minsk

    Five years at the Belarusian State University of Informatics and Radioelectronics. My first real success in futsal was making the university team.

    To 2007 · Minsk

  4. 2006

    Into Itransition, and into a captain's armband

    Out of BSUIR and straight into Itransition, where I ended up captaining the company futsal team. That team is the reason everything after it happened. The year is from my own memory: the first documented tournament for the company team is 2010.

    Itransition

  5. 2007

    I started scouting myself

    A ten-skill rubric I wrote in a notebook and scored daily out of five: endurance, acceleration, scoring, shooting, weaker foot, field vision, long passing, dribbling, tackling and pivot play, with a written verdict every week. One of them reads: weak at game play, acceleration, endurance; strong at shooting, pivot play, self-training and analysis. On the same pages I wrote down the goal of being a great footballer, and the idea that later became EasyChamp.

    To 2008 · Notebook

  6. 2008

    The first thing I wrote about my own passing

    In a player-evaluation notebook from a session at BATU I scored seven players in two independent columns, mine and Dima Yarmolich's, and wrote about myself: played creatively organising attacks, penetrating passes («обостряющие передачи», obostryayushchie peredachi). That is still the clearest description of what I do.

    BATU · Notebook

  7. 2009

    European champion, Nantes

    IBSA B2/B3 European Championship, Nantes, France, with the Belarus national futsal team for the visually impaired. Eleven teams, July 2009. Belarus beat Ukraine 4-0 in the decisive match.

    France · Gold

  8. 2009

    Master of Sport of the Republic of Belarus

    18 November 2009, futsal among the visually impaired, by the Ministry of Sport and Tourism of the Republic of Belarus. The identity booklet is photographed and reproduced on the gallery, so this line is graded on a document rather than on recollection.

    Belarus · Title

  9. 2009

    Commendation of the President of the Republic of Belarus

    16 December 2009, by order signed by Alexander Lukashenko, for the win in Nantes. Eleven players and the coach, with my name first on the list. A state award by signed order.

    Belarus · Order

  10. 2010

    The Itransition futsal team founded

    I founded the company futsal team inside Itransition and ran it as captain and manager. First outing was the annual IT Spartakiad football tournament: second of eight teams. By the autumn the club was in the national top division: it lost its debut 1-2 on 11 September 2010 and won for the first time on 25 September, 5-3 against Lidselmash.

    Itransition

  11. 2010/11

    Minsk city futsal championship, won

    In my own published words at the time: completely unexpectedly, for everyone and even for ourselves, we took first place in the city championship. That title is what got us the invitation into the national league. The interview does not fix the calendar year, so this one sits on the season rather than on a single year.

    Minsk · Title

  12. 2011

    Belarus national top futsal division, debut

    Itransition-BGUFK, ninth of fifteen in the Vysshaya Liga, with me as its founder, captain and manager. We allied with BGUFK, the Belarusian State University of Physical Culture, for a training base, a methodology and squad depth; the cooperation agreement was signed on 8 August 2011. Also that year: ALF Minsk Vtoraya Liga, 11-a-side, with Meteor, shirt 4. Eight appearances, six starts, 410 minutes; team fifth of sixteen.

    Vysshaya Liga

  13. 2011

    World Games in Antalya, the squad's title

    2011 IBSA World Games, Antalya: the Belarus squad won the world title; my own note from the time says the medical commission did not clear me that year. This is the Turkey trophy that gets confused with the one a year later in Kayseri, which I did play in.

    Antalya · The squad's title

  14. 2012

    Fifth in the Belarus national top futsal division

    Itransition-BGUFK finished fifth in the Vysshaya Liga. That is the national top division, not a city league. In the ALF Minsk Pervaya Liga, 11-a-side, with Exiteq (Экситек), shirt 4: thirteen appearances, eleven starts, 655 minutes; team fourth of sixteen and promoted. Fourth place is a promotion, not a title.

    Belarus

  15. 2012

    Five in a game, two in the Cup, Kayseri, and the first bench

    Five goals in one match: Itransition 15-4 VRZ, 13 November, Sportcomplex BGUFK, goals at 10, 19, 25, 48 and 56 minutes. Across that Minsk city championship season 27 full protocols survive and I score in 18 of them. Two goals in a Belarus Cup last-32 tie, 16 December, for Rudakovo (Рудаково): Gorki 8-4 Rudakovo after extra time, both of mine putting us ahead. In June I was with the Belarus squad in Kayseri, Turkey, for the IBSA B2/B3 European Championship of 4 to 9 June, which Belarus won. I played, and I have the gold medal from it, photographed on the final day. The medal is documented; the minutes are my own account. And my first coaching spell: a tournament-prep futsal side, seven dated session plans, an opening address, and a per-player shot and goal logging method that later turned into EasyChamp.

    Minsk · Kayseri

  16. 2013

    Top-flight futsal and the ALF top division

    ALF Vysshaya Liga, 11-a-side, with Exiteq, shirt 4: fifteen appearances, eight starts, 685 minutes, one goal and one assist; team fifth of fourteen. On 26 October, a Belarus Vysshaya Liga away appearance: MFK Granit (Mikashevichi) 6-3 MFK Rudakovo, Dvorets kultury Granit, 200 spectators, in the official line-up, second line, coach Sergei Klyashtorny. My first goal in the national top flight came for Rudakovo away at Bazar (Luninets) on 23 March: the opener on 10 minutes in a 5-5 draw, round 21, and the clip is on the highlights page. Bazar was the opponent, never one of my teams.

    Rudakovo · Exiteq

  17. 2014

    ALF Vysshaya Liga silver

    Second of fourteen with Exiteq in the ALF Minsk top division, 11-a-side: 26 games, 19 wins, 2 draws, 5 defeats, 73-43, 59 points. My line: shirt 4, ten appearances, two starts, 296 minutes.

    Minsk · Silver

  18. 2014

    I started the Business League and the IT League myself

    September 2014. Three award ceremonies followed over the next six months. This is the pre-history of EasyChamp. The 2014/15 season is also the earliest full season of mine that survives on video as a compilation.

    Minsk · Organiser

  19. 2015

    A full international, and a Team of the Round

    Belarus against France, visually-impaired national teams, Borisov, May 2015. Six years after Nantes, still in the national-team picture. Team of the Round («Сборная тура») selection on 18 January: Arena 6-7 FC Dinamit, Fanfutzal, three goals and two assists. In the ALF Vysshaya Liga 11-a-side with Exiteq, shirt 36: twelve appearances, five starts, 554 minutes, two goals and one assist; team tenth of sixteen.

    Borisov · Minsk

  20. 2015

    Second coaching spell

    A Minsk city-championship futsal side. A written self-introduction as coach, a scripted tactics lecture built as theory then drills then practice, and three structured plus-minus post-match reviews. Recorded result: we beat Gorchitsa by one goal.

    Minsk · Bench

I had minus ten glasses, but to take part I had to pass every eye test on special equipment. I passed, I qualified, and we won the Euro.

Anton AbyzovOn Nantes 2009.

Part two2016 to 2026

Minsk to Germany to Miami.

  1. 2016

    A quiet year on paper

    ALF Vysshaya Liga 11-a-side with Exiteq, one appearance, 15 minutes; team ninth of sixteen. Itransition were still playing: the Liga Mini-futbola match against Poliklinika on 24 November is on film.

    Minsk

  2. 2017

    Fifteen goals in the ALF futsal top division

    ALF Mini-football Vysshaya Liga 2017/18 with Exiteq: 15 goals, 1 assist, 1 best-player award. My best single ALF futsal return. Registered for the ALF 11-a-side top division but played no minutes; left Exiteq on 26 July 2017, team fifth of sixteen.

    Exiteq · Futsal

  3. 2017

    Back in the national top division, with Svetlogorsk

    2017/18, six years after Itransition: seven appearances for Svetlogorsk in the Belarus top futsal division. The official BAMF yearbook for the XXIX championship, 2 September 2017 to 2 June 2018, sixteen clubs, lists me in the Svetlogorsk squad with seven.

    Svetlogorsk · Vysshaya Liga

  4. 2018

    The year the small pitch paid

    SLMF COMEBACK-2018 with MFK Titan: 18 goals, 4 assists, 22 goal contributions, seventh top scorer of the league. Team third of fourteen: 13 played, 9 wins, 1 draw, 3 defeats, 117-51. Four goals in one match on 19 August, JOMA BAND 9-9 MFK Titan at MGTSOR Atlet, at 10, 12, 35 and 37 minutes. That is the one external Belarus protocol still live that names me goal by goal.

    SLMF · Titan

  5. 2018

    Twelve goals and nine assists in ALF summer 5x5

    ALF summer 5x5 championship with Exiteq: 12 goals, 9 assists, one best-player award.

    ALF · 5x5

  6. 2018

    ALF 8x8 championship silver with Blugrana

    Team second of eight; my line 4 goals, 3 assists, one best-player award, shirt 25. The 8x8 format is the closest thing to 6v6 in my official record.

    Blugrana · Silver

  7. 2018

    CrossFit, and the engine behind the attendance

    My first CrossFit session was in early August 2018, after trying it twice on a trip to Italy, and my calves hurt for more than a week afterwards. About 60 dated session notes follow, dense from April 2021 to February 2022, in a private Minsk training group. Three CrossFit Opens are on file: 21.1 on 12 March 2021 with 209 double-unders, 21.2 on 22 March 2021 in 17:19 inside the twenty minute cap and on a 60 cm box rather than the standard 50, and 22.1 on 28 February 2022 while I was unwell. Cindy came out at 21 rounds plus 17, DT at 7:53. Heaviest lifts recorded: a 140 kg deadlift on 24 March 2021 and an 83 kg front squat in March 2023. Before any of that, about 50 dated gym logs between February 2014 and August 2015. On 25 April 2021 I went to an inter-club crossfit competition in Minsk, and in 2019 I ran a half marathon on bib 393. No placing and no finish time are claimed for either, because the timing page will not give one up. At 41 I am still training, and that is what stands behind 100 per cent attendance across seven EasyChamp seasons.

    Off the pitch

  8. 2019

    Scoring against the reigning Belarus champion

    17 February, Vysshaya Liga: Territory-Maxline 5-11 Lidselmash, SK MAPID, Minsk, 16:30, 220 spectators. Official line, my goal at 13 minutes from Popovich's pass, plus my pre-assist on the opening own goal. Booked at 14 minutes. Second line: Eliseev, Syrokvash, Abyzov, Popovich, shirt 3. Lidselmash were the reigning Belarus champions, four-time national champions, and had played the Elite Round of the 2018/19 UEFA Futsal Champions League three months earlier against Kairat Almaty and Chrudim. Also that year: MFK Territory-Maxline against MFK Granit, Vysshaya Liga, 5 May.

    Vysshaya Liga

  9. 2019

    Twenty-six goals in twenty games for Belgiprozem

    Season 2019/20, Minsk city championship (BAMF) with Belgiprozem: 26 goals and 10 assists in 20 games. Two independent counts agree, my own season summary and the sum of the 20 match notes. Best game: Belgiprozem 10-2 MFK MTST on 16 December, five goals and an assist. The season was cut short by COVID. One footnote I keep in my own file: on 17 January 2020 the first goal was credited to me though I did not score it, so the honest figure that season is 25 or 26, not 26 flat.

    To 2020 · BAMF

  10. 2020

    Six in a game, and a season cut in half

    Six goals in one match plus best player on 14 September: Scout team Belarus 11-6 Street Boys, Premierliga 5x5 Summer Cup. My own caveat: Street Boys were largely Disa players staying on after their own game, the public match page is dead and it was never archived. Five goals and two assists plus best player on 3 December: Blugrana 17-4 Maglef in the ALF, two twenty-minute halves. The Belarus visually-impaired national championship in Grodno on 30 December is on film.

    Minsk · Grodno

  11. 2021

    The biggest year in the archive

    48 logged matches, 81 goals, 28 assists, 13 best-player mentions. Teams that season: BelVEB, FC Standart, MPR, ISsoft, Belgiprozem-2, and the visually-impaired side. Plus 9 goals and 2 best-player awards in the ALF Mini-football Bazovaya Liga Major with Blugrana.

    Minsk · Recorded

  12. 2021

    The IT Cup final equaliser

    12 August: ISsoft 3-2 Luch. I scored the equaliser, first time, in open play, on a three-against-three break, at 2501 seconds of the broadcast. The stream title carries the word «Финал». Earlier that year, on 29 May, I reached the Korobka Cup final with FC Standart and scored in a 2-3 defeat to INTEAM.

    IT Cup · ISsoft

  13. 2021

    Seven goals and four assists in one match

    14 December, Territory Futbola. The single biggest line in the archive. No external record of it survives, the league's match page is now a casino domain. The rest of that year: six goals plus best player on 10 April, FC Standart 9-5 Orlayn in the Korobka Cup; four goals and three assists plus best player on 25 March, Jenty 4-10 BelVEB; a free-kick goal on 11 December in ISSOFT 12-1 Ozimye, three goals and two assists; two sombrero flicks on the touchline on 18 April plus a cross that forced an own goal; and on 16 October I went in goal after we conceded six, and still scored in a four-against-four.

    Territory Futbola

  14. 2022

    Germany: the only goal, and man of the match

    10 April: SV Rangsdorf 28 1-0 SV Siethen, Kreisfreundschaftsspiel, Kreis Dahme/Flaming, Brandenburg, Stadion Rangsdorfer See, 45 spectators, kick-off 15:00. I scored the only goal and Rangsdorf's coach Michi named me man of the match. The official fixture page records one goal event at 57 minutes. This is German district amateur football, and that is how it should be described.

    Brandenburg

  15. 2022

    Florida, from August

    January and February were the last Belarus block, ISsoft friendlies, including a game I logged as very intense with a lot of dribbling. June, Madrid: a pickup game against a Colombian side, filmed. May, Barcelona: the Spanish futsal LaLiga at Camp Nou, as a spectator. Then Florida. I picked the region more or less at random and then texted every soccer team in Miami I could find until one tryout answered. The first month on synthetic grass my calves hurt so badly I had to walk through sessions and could not take part in the trial. That thread is what led to Joe Rotbart and Dade County FC.

    Miami

  16. 2023

    UPSL Premier Division, Dade County FC

    Registered and playing as a UPSL Premier Division player with Dade County FC, Spring and Fall. Two signed instruments in my name: a Release of Liability, and a Premier Player Acceptance Letter for the 2023 Fall Season, plus the same letter for Spring. On 13 May, UPSL Premier: Mako Soccer Club 4-3 Dade County FC, Port St. Lucie, away. I assisted the first DCFC goal, to Daniel Revivo. The public box score lists no assists, so that one is my own account. I played the 2024 season for the club as well, and I am registered with them again for Fall 2026 in UPSL Division 1.

    UPSL · Miami

  17. 2023

    Nomadas, then UKG Dream Team

    Earliest club footage is two Veo matches from February 2023. A club practice video from 10 October has me in the number 11 red bib, blocking a shot. October and November: Nomadas in the Miami Futsal League, Doral, with an MVP in a 7-6 win over Santile on 6 November; then UKG Dream Team 8v8 in Liga Evolution, shirt 9, including a brace against Nicoya FC.

    Doral · Miami

  18. 2024

    A coaching badge on the announcement, and a league won

    Spring 2024: announced publicly as a coach of Dade County FC, assisting head coach Artem Baranovskyi, and still on the UPSL roster as a player that season. The club's wording: two people who know the club in and out. Dade County FC won the USSL in 2024. The owner's own message to the squad: you have shown you are well above the USSL level, winning the league with ease. First season at Traz Powell Stadium, last game 30 June, and no post-season was played, so there was no final. I am photographed with the trophy on the pitch at Traz Powell that night, 30 June 2024.

    Miami · League won

  19. 2024

    EasyChamp FC starts playing

    April 2024: my own 8v8 club in Miami starts playing. First documented match 3 April. Across seven PS23 seasons that followed it is about 88 games, and the club's own records put me at 100 per cent attendance in every season that was measured, registered Midfield and Admin, captain.

    Miami · 8v8

  20. 2024

    Gol Gala champions in Miami, and a 3,000 dollar team prize

    23 June 2024: we won the Gol Gala tournament in Miami. The oversized cheque handed over on the pitch reads GOL GALA CHAMPIONS, June 23 2024, three thousand and 00/100 dollars, signed TheSportSpectator, and the cup is in the same frame. Pink Gol Gala kit; two more frames from that afternoon show the same shirt in play.

    Miami · Prize

  21. 2024

    Four goals in the week of my fortieth birthday

    November 2024: four goals in a 5-1 win over Five Stars FC, one of the toughest teams in Liga Corporativa La Red, for End It Soccer X Spanish. December 2024 into February 2025 with the same side at La Redkocha: a free-kick goal on 16 December, a goal at 49:55 for Dream House in Liga Evolution on 13 December, and an assist with a feint at 43:45 in a Miami 8v8 game on 2 December.

    Miami · La Red

  22. 2025

    Championship Clubs League 5x5 title, North Miami

    With Team Patagonia. In my own public words: had the honour of playing with Team Patagonia in the Championship Clubs League 5x5 in North Miami, and we took home the title.

    North Miami · Title

  23. 2025

    Miami Superliga 8v8 champions with EasyChamp FC

    July 2025, with the club I own, coach and play in, shirt 8. The season read 11 wins, 1 draw, 4 defeats, 96 goals for and 61 against, 34 points. I allocated a 3,000 dollar prize pool across the squad myself. PS23 stopped publishing per-player scorers after season C92, so I cannot tell you how many of those 96 were mine.

    Miami · Champions

  24. 2025

On 2 May, Gulfstream FC 7-2 Miami All Stars, Hallandale Beach, 8v8: one goal, a header.

    Miami · 8v8

  25. 2025

    Seventeen goals back in Minsk

One detail from that season: the FC Flexy-2 side I faced in July 2025 contained four of my own Territory-Maxline top-flight teammates from the 2019 Lidselmash protocol, six years on. August 2025: FONBET ALF summer 5x5 championship with Exiteq, 4 goals, 1 assist, one best-player award, plus three games for Alo Ref. And on 15 November, Gulfstream FC in Liga Evolution Fecha 9 against 8 Machos: the low finish off a corner routine is at 2415 seconds of the stream.

The near goal is mostly outside the camera's frame that night, so nothing else from it is counted.

    Miami · 8v8

  27. 2026

Team second in the MAPID group on 23 points, one behind bam-servis.by. Inside it: three goals and two assists plus best player of the match and of the team in KAPITAN 0-11 EasyChamp on 25 June; five goals in EasyChamp 12-0 KAPITAN on 6 August; best player in EasyChamp 7-3 BERN on 16 July; one goal, one assist and a five-against-four defensive stand in the 6-6 with bam-servis.by on 30 July.

  30. 2026

    UPSL Division 1
